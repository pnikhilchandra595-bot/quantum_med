## 2. Components & Modular Breakdown

### Component 1: Data Ingestion & Preprocessing
- **Purpose:** Loads benchmark medical datasets, handles missing clinical measurements, applies z-score normalization, and creates stratified train/validation folds.
- **Location:** `src/data/`
- **Files:**
  - `wdbc_loader.py` — Wisconsin Breast Cancer loader (569 samples, 30 features).
  - `heart_loader.py` — UCI Cleveland Heart Disease loader (303 samples, 13 features).
  - `splitter.py` — Stratified 5-fold cross-validation index generator.

### Component 2: Feature Selection & Quantum Encoding
- **Purpose:** Compresses high-dimensional clinical spaces to 4–8 features and scales them to $[-\pi, \pi]$ to match quantum simulator budgets.
- **Location:** `src/features/`
- **Files:**
  - `pca_reducer.py` — Principal Component Analysis with explained variance tracking.
  - `mi_selector.py` — Non-linear Mutual Information feature selection preserving clinical names.

### Component 3: Quantum Circuit Engine & Simulator Manager
- **Purpose:** Manages PennyLane devices, quantum feature maps, and parameterized variational circuits with barren-plateau mitigation.
- **Location:** `src/quantum/`
- **Files:**
  - `backend.py` — Device factory for `default.qubit`, `qasm_simulator`, and $N \le 8$ qubit safety bounds.
  - `feature_maps.py` — AngleEmbedding and Second-Order Pauli-Z ($ZZ$-FeatureMap) with CNOT-RZ-CNOT entanglement.
  - `ansatz.py` — StronglyEntanglingLayers, BasicEntangler, and Gaussian parameter initializers.

### Component 4: Hybrid Quantum Models
- **Purpose:** Quantum-enhanced estimators with scikit-learn compatible APIs.
- **Location:** `src/models/quantum/`
- **Files:**
  - `qsvc.py` — Quantum Kernel SVM with fidelity transition probability Gram matrix $K_{ij}$.
  - `vqc.py` — Variational Quantum Classifier with parameter-shift Adam optimization.
  - `hqnn.py` — Hybrid Quantum Neural Network coupling PyTorch with PennyLane `TorchLayer`.

### Component 5: Explainability (XAI)

## 3. Data Flow
1. **Raw Medical Ingestion:** User or CLI triggers dataset load (`load_wdbc()` or `load_heart_disease()`).
2. **Stratified Splitting:** 5-fold CV splits indices without data leakage.
3. **Quantum Dimensionality Reduction:** Features compressed to $k \in [4, 8]$ dimensions and scaled to $[-\pi, \pi]$.
4. **Quantum Embedding & Execution:**
   - QSVC: Samples mapped to quantum states via $U_{\Phi(x)}$ to evaluate Gram matrix.
   - VQC/HQNN: Variational parameters $\theta$ optimized via parameter-shift gradient descent.
5. **Evaluation & Explainability:** Metrics calculated across folds; KernelSHAP waterfall plots generated for clinical decision support.

## 4. Technical Debt & Conventions
- **Naming:** Snake_case for functions and filenames, PascalCase for estimator classes (`QuantumSupportVectorClassifier`).
- **Qubit Safety:** Hard cap of $N \le 8$ qubits in `src/quantum/backend.py` to prevent $O(2^N)$ RAM exhaustion on laptops.
- **Deterministic Reproducibility:** Fixed seeds across NumPy, PyTorch, and PennyLane.
